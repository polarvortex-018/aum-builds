Passive Cooling Systems

India's climate demands that buildings breathe, not just shelter. Our site intelligence process begins by mapping the thermal envelope before design begins — orienting buildings to prevailing breezes, shading critical facades from the western sun, and using the earth's thermal mass to buffer indoor temperatures.

Core passive strategies include cross-ventilation corridors calculated by AI wind simulation, deep overhangs sized to the site's latitude, and earth-berming on west-facing walls in hot-dry zones. In warm-humid climates like coastal Karnataka, we prioritise stack ventilation and high thermal emissivity roofing materials.

ECBC 2017 Compliance

India's Energy Conservation Building Code 2017 mandates passive design strategies including daylight access, shading, and thermal envelope performance — and we treat ECBC as a floor, not a ceiling. Our AI simultaneously evaluates prescriptive compliance and whole-building performance simulation, often achieving Super ECBC performance (40–45% more efficient than conventional buildings) without additional cost.

  • Building envelope trade-off optimisation — AI models hundreds of wall/window/insulation combinations
  • Climate zone calibration — pre-loaded with all 5 Indian climate zones: Composite, Hot & Dry, Warm & Humid, Moderate, and Cold
  • Daylight simulation — AI calculates useful daylight illuminance for every room, reducing artificial lighting loads by up to 50%

Vastu & Solar Alignment

Vastu Shastra is a pre-scientific climate-responsive design system developed over millennia in the subcontinent. East-west orientation maximises morning light and minimises afternoon heat gain. We overlay Vastu analysis with solar path data and wind rose diagrams so every spatial decision has both scientific and traditional validation.

What We Deliver

A Site Intelligence Report: terrain analysis, solar path animation, wind corridor mapping, flood zone overlay, Vastu axis study, and ECBC compliance projection — delivered before design begins.

AI-Assisted Massing and Spatial Configuration

Traditional architecture produces 3–5 design options before presenting to a client. We generate hundreds. Using parametric modelling and AI-driven form-finding, our system explores the full range of spatial configurations that satisfy a site's constraints simultaneously.

Each generated configuration is automatically evaluated against passive performance metrics: daylighting adequacy, cross-ventilation potential, solar shading, and ECBC envelope benchmarks. Only the top-performing forms are surfaced for human review.

Vernacular Intelligence

India has thousands of years of climate-responsive building tradition. Our AI models are trained on vernacular typologies — the courtyard house of Rajasthan, the sloped roofs of Kerala, the thick-walled adobe of the Deccan plateau — as well as contemporary performance data.

  • Courtyard proportioning for natural ventilation and thermal buffering
  • Roof geometry optimised for rainwater harvesting and solar shading
  • Section design for stack ventilation in warm-humid climates
  • Thick-wall construction logic for hot-dry zones — walls as thermal batteries

From Concept to Construction Documents at 4× Speed

The 4× speed advantage comes from AI handling the computational work that used to require weeks of manual drafting: structural coordination, documentation, quantity take-offs, and code compliance checking.

The Problem with Conventional Material Specification

Most architects specify materials by habit, brand familiarity, or catalogue — without analysing thermal performance, embodied carbon, local availability, or regional tradition. The result is buildings that perform poorly for the climate and sever the connection to their landscape.

AI-Driven Material Cross-Reference

Our material intelligence system simultaneously evaluates four dimensions for every specification decision: thermal performance, embodied carbon, local sourcing potential (within 200km), and vernacular precedent.

  • Rammed earth and compressed earth blocks — thermal mass, zero embodied carbon, locally sourceable across most of India
  • AAC blocks and fly ash bricks — lightweight, good insulation, made from industrial waste
  • uPVC double-glazed windows — ECBC-compliant fenestration with high thermal resistance
  • Cool roofs — high solar reflectance membranes that reduce cooling load by up to 28%
  • Bamboo composite — structural and finish applications with exceptional embodied carbon credentials
  • Local stone — Kota, Kadappa, Shahabad, Cuddapah — each mapped by our AI to climate zones

GRIHA and Embodied Carbon Compliance

Our AI generates a GRIHA projection at the specification stage — before construction begins — so clients know their sustainability credentials before a single brick is laid. We target GRIHA 4-star as standard, with 5-star achievable on most projects.
