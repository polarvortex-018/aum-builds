— Who We Are

Two minds.
One direction.

Aum_buIlds is a young, future-forward practice that believes architecture must think before it is built. We combine environmental AI with deep design sensitivity to create spaces that perform — and endure.

Founder & Creative Director
Ar. Poorvi Dasari
AI-Assisted Environmental Architect
"The land already knows what the building should be. Our work is simply to listen carefully enough."

Poorvi founded Aum_buIlds on a conviction that architecture's most urgent task is learning to work with nature — not against it. Trained in architecture and environmental design, she built the practice around a single philosophical premise: every site already holds the intelligence for its building. The role of the architect is to surface it.

She integrates AI-assisted analysis with deep vernacular knowledge, bringing together computational environmental modelling and the spatial intuition shaped by India's rich building traditions. Her designs are simultaneously data-precise and emotionally resonant — buildings that feel as though they have always belonged to their land.

Poorvi leads creative direction, environmental strategy, and every project's design intelligence from first site reading to final form.

Head of Design Operations
Nandni Padmakumar
Client Strategy & Design Intelligence Lead
"Great architecture lives in the relationship between the designer, the client, and the space they're trying to create together."

Nandni is the connective intelligence of Aum_buIlds — the person who translates a client's vision into a project that can actually be built, loved, and lived in. Her intuition for people and design makes her the bridge between Poorvi's environmental vision and the human reality of every project.

She leads the full client experience: from the first conversation and project brief through design coordination, stakeholder management, and delivery. Her approach is collaborative by nature and strategic by instinct — she understands that the best buildings emerge from genuine partnership between architect and inhabitant.

Nandni brings operational intelligence, design sensitivity, and the quiet clarity that keeps complex projects moving toward their most meaningful outcomes.
